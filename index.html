Executive Summary

The renewal window is the opening

Nissan's ServiceNow agreement is a 5-year contract that expires in March 2027, and renegotiation is underway now. We reviewed NNA's current ServiceNow footprint in detail: licensing, configuration items, and module usage. Most of it can move to Salesforce IT Service at a fraction of the cost, with simpler licensing and AI-driven ticket deflection built in.

Bottom line

At industry-standard pricing, we estimate 80–90% of NNA's current ServiceNow licensing could be eliminated by moving to Salesforce IT Service. Hold off on any long-term ServiceNow renewal. Take a one-year bridge renewal at most, and run a parallel proof of value in the meantime.

That creates real exposure. Current ITOM licensing (3,820 units) looks short by roughly 3×–6× against the real managed estate (~17K CIs, or ~47K including endpoints). A gap that size triggers a large, unplanned true-up at renewal, which is exactly why we'd renegotiate scope and pricing model rather than renew as-is.

A two-step fix — and it holds regardless of which platform NNA ends up running

Rationalize the data

Retire the 89% of ephemeral cloud VM and batch noise so the CMDB reflects what NNA actually manages.

Simplify the pricing model

Move to a flat per-CI model, with no per-class subscription meters and no coupling to autoscaled cloud VM counts, so the bill stops moving every time the infrastructure autoscales.

45%→80%

The differentiator here is AI ticket deflection

A comparable-sized customer saw roughly 45% ticket deflection after adopting Agentforce for IT agents — mostly password resets, hardware requests, and similar high-volume, low-complexity tickets. That number climbed month over month as the knowledge base matured, and more mature customers are seeing up to 80% deflection.

Recommended Path Forward

Protect the leverage; prove it out

Given the renewal timing, we'd steer NNA away from locking into another long-term agreement before this gets a real test.

Keep any renewal to one year, not a multi-year term

A long renewal now gives away NNA's leverage and forecloses this option before it's proven out.

Run a parallel proof of value on the core ITSM + CMDB workload

We'll evaluate the past 12–18 months of ServiceNow ticket data, simulate a live production environment, and produce a report on ticket deflection and related savings. Salesforce will also run discovery to determine the licensing cost savings, which we expect to be substantial.

Use the CMDB rationalization exercise as a forcing function — whatever platform NNA picks

Cleaning up the 89% of noise CIs reduces the license true-up exposure either way and makes any go-forward pricing, ServiceNow or Salesforce, more defensible.

Treat SecOps and legacy GRC as a separate, longer-horizon conversation

Keep them out of the critical path rather than letting them block moving the ITSM / CMDB core now.

Either way, this gives NNA a credible, evidence-based alternative to a multi-year ServiceNow renewal — and a stronger negotiating position.
